# Guard audit — what else is speculative machinery?

Read-only sweep of every non-test file in `src/`, against the doctrine: **trusted intra-project
clients, no malicious-client defense, delete speculative machinery** — but **keep every guard that
encodes a real platform behaviour or a real observed incident**.

A guard here = any check, timeout, assert, dedupe, sweep, retry, refusal, canonicality assertion,
race protection, WeakSet/Map bookkeeping or error branch whose purpose is to defend a scenario
rather than implement a feature. `providedAtOffset` is already handled in
`itx-surface-SYNTHESIS.md` §7 and is not re-litigated here.

Headline: **~95 lines of speculative guard** across 20 sites, of which five are worth a commit.
The bulk of what looks defensive in this package turns out to be load-bearing platform knowledge —
the KEEP table below is the more useful half of this document.

## Notes

**A. `assertLiveValue` and `#sweepPending` are the same guard twice.** `lendStubOverRelay`
reserves a transport (`rpcStubAttach`) _before_ it calls `provider.dup()`. So a non-stub `target`
burns a reservation and then throws — which is exactly the abandoned reservation the sweep exists
to collect. Swap `rpc-stub-relay.ts:113` and `:114` and both guards lose their scenario at once.
Delete only one of the two and the leak stays reachable.

**B. `#pendingDials` and "no upgrade leg arrived" are one interlock**, not two guards — the gate
admits the leg, the check confirms it arrived. Delete them together or keep them together.

**C. The `parse`/`dispatch` `__proto__` pair is a duplicate, not defence in depth.** Expressions
are persisted names; the refusal that matters is at the point of _use_ (`stepGet`), because a
hand-appended `capability-provided` event never passes through `parse`'s call site.

**D. Idempotent-no-op reduces are features, not guards** — `core-processor.ts:206-207,225,231,249`
returning `undefined` on a benign double-revoke/remove is what keeps a reconnect at zero checkpoint
writes and zero live-state deltas. Left alone.
